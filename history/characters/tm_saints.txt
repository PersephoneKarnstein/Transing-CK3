# Crusader Dolls — the five foremothers of Adelphai Dorleyaiou, the faith's own saints.
# Invented characters, not historical people: named after the women of Alyson Greaves's Welcome to Dorley Hall,
# in Byzantine form (docs/faith_design.md §16; "Diana" keeps her own name, user 2026-10-21). All died before 867.
# Lowborn, as vanilla's saints without a known house are. Their lives are in their epithets' _desc loc
# (common/nicknames/tm_saints_nicknames.txt, localization/english/tm_saints_l_english.yml).
# At the 867 start each is enshrined at a holy site of the faith, one saint per province, as vanilla does for
# its own pre-start saints (common/scripted_effects/tm_saints_effects.txt). Fallback tomb: Midaion (province 5562),
# the church barony of Phrygia.

# Stephania, the First Mother: founded the House at Dorylaion; the first Mother of Mothers.
tm_saint_stephania = {
	name = "Stephania"
	female = yes
	culture = kybeline
	religion = transbian_maximalism
	trait = tm_daughter_of_the_knife
	trait = education_learning_4
	trait = brave

		}
