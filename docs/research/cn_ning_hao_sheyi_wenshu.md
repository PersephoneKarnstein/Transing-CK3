## 1. Argument

### What the introduction says (前言)

- **What a she was.** She "是中國古代的一種基層社會組織" ("a grassroots social organisation of ancient China"), continuous from pre-Qin times to the modern era. The edition offers nearly 400 documents to study Tang to early-Song she, "主要是私社" ("mostly private she") (p. 1).
- **Research history** (pp. 1–7).
  - Naba Toshisada's three types are kept as "具有啟發性" ("still suggestive"): she organised by Buddhist believers around monasteries; mutual-aid groups formed by commoners at home (在家); and mixed she that did both (p. 2).
  - The editors fault Naba on two counts. He treated *every* call to a she member as Buddhist service to a monastery, when the spring and autumn feast circulars were ordinary notices. And he dated she to Buddhist associations, when "私社早在漢代即已出現" ("private she already existed in the Han") (p. 3).
  - Gernet's 1956 chapter is credited with "the traditional she can turn into Buddhist associations under certain conditions", which gives clues to the historical fact of not separating 邑 and 社 (p. 4).
- **The corpus** (p. 7). The editors gathered 396 documents. They removed 53 duplicates and added 2 from Turfan, for **345 items**.
- **Two broad classes** (p. 8).
  - 實用文書, documents actually used.
  - 非實用, which covers three kinds:
    - 文樣, model texts;
    - 稿, drafts;
    - 抄, copies, often by student scribes (學郎) "隨手書寫" ("jotted down") and so error-prone.
- **The five document types** (p. 9): 社條 (bylaws), 社司轉帖 (circulars), 社曆 (accounts), 社文 (liturgical texts) and 社狀牒 (petitions and reports).
  - **Bylaws:** 20 collected and 18 kept. Eight are established as actual working bylaws. One copy is a student's model for "成人結社習俗" ("the custom of adults forming she") (p. 9).
    - A working bylaw copied a model and "按具體情況作一些變動" ("adapted it to the situation"). The example given is **S.527**, which "顯然是依據這個文樣草成" ("was obviously drafted from" the model 上祖社條 S.6537v) (p. 10).
    - The founding 大條 / 祖條 was sealed (封印). Bylaws were re-made "因戰亂造成社邑活動停頓，或社邑成員有較大變動（死亡、離散、新入社、退社等）" ("when war halted the she, or membership changed greatly: deaths, dispersal, new members, withdrawals"). S.527 and P.3544 are the examples (pp. 10–11).
  - **Circulars:** 218 collected, 193 kept, of which only 60-odd are real used copies. Members marked receipt with 知 or an ink dot, and the office ticked attendance (p. 12).
    - Typology by occasion (pp. 13–14):
      - death (身亡, 33 kept);
      - spring and autumn feasts (春座/秋座局席, 61);
      - 建福 / 設齋 / 設供 (23);
      - "small matters" meetings (少事商量, 15);
      - extra levies and feasts (6);
      - unclear (48);
      - channel she and channel workers' circulars (35).
    - Death circulars are "唐後期五代宋初敦煌社邑最重要的活動" ("the most important activity" of the she) (p. 13).
  - **Accounts (社曆):** 43 (p. 15).
    - Funeral-gift ledgers (身故納贈曆, 29) list each member's contribution at a death of a member "或其親屬" ("or their kin").
    - Nine **便物曆**, loan ledgers. Six of these are certainly the she office lending "麵、油、粟、麥、黃麻" ("flour, oil, millet, wheat, hemp") to members. They give evidence for "敦煌私社的公共積累『義聚』" ("the common accumulation of Dunhuang private she, called **yiju 義聚**") (p. 15).
  - **Liturgical texts (社文):** 93 collected, 68 kept, in eight sorts. They include lamp-lighting texts, sand-stamped Buddha texts, merit records, and texts for the she sacrifice. These are texts for the sacrifice to the god of the soil, partly modelled on the Kaiyuan ritual code (pp. 16–21).
  - **Petitions (狀牒):** 22 kept. They include applications to join (投社狀) and to leave (退社狀, only one survives). "各社社條一般都對社人退社規定了較為嚴厲的罰則" ("bylaws generally set fairly severe penalties for leaving") (p. 21).
- **Official and private she** (pp. 21–22). Only a few official she (官社) appear, such as the horse she (馬社) of the Kaiyuan-era Xuanquan prefecture. "敦煌寫本社邑文書的絕大多數是私社文書" ("the great majority are private she documents"), which reflects their flourishing in Tang to early-Song Dunhuang.
- **Dating** (p. 22). Only 21 documents carry era names, from Kaiyuan 14 (726) to Taiping xingguo 7 (982). Eighty-five carry cyclical dates. The editors date 197 documents in all.

### Every women's association document in the edition

The table of contents has two documents titled 女人社 and one ledger of 社家女人. The appendix adds two laywomen's (優婆夷) associations.

**(1) S.527, 顯德六年（959）正月三日女人社社條 (doc. 7, pp. 23–26).** The full text is in `cn_s527_womens_association.md`; the corrections are below.
- The editors' 說明: the she members "重新製定的社條" ("re-made the bylaws") in the first month of 959. Like P.3544, this was because war interrupted the she or membership changed: deaths, dispersal, new entries and withdrawals (p. 26).
- **It is adapted from a men's model.** In 上祖社條 (doc. 17, S.6537v/7-8) the line reads "大者如兄，少者若弟" ("the older as elder brothers, the younger as younger brothers") (p. 55). S.527 has "大者若姊，小者若妹" (p. 24). The editors' 說明 to doc. 17: S.527's preamble "與本件基本相同，後面的具體規定文字有的與本件也相同" ("is basically the same as this one, and some later clauses too") (p. 57).
- **The women set softer penalties than the model.**

  | | Model 上祖社條 (p. 56) | Women's she, S.527 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Leaving the she | 各決丈(杖)卅棒, "thirty strokes each", then a feast | three strokes (參棒), then a feast |
  | Feasts of the year | Ullambana on the fifteenth of the seventh month (七月十五日造于蘭盆) and the spring and autumn feasts (p. 55) | New Year 建福 only |

**(2) P.3489, 戊辰年（968?）正月廿四日䄠坊巷女人社社條 (doc. 8, pp. 27–28).** This bylaw was **not in the existing notes**.
- **Text** (my punctuation follows the edition):
  - 戊辰年正月廿四日，䄠坊巷女人團座商儀(議)立條。合社商量為定。
  - 各自榮(營)生死者，納麵壹㪷，須得齊同，不得怠慢。或若怠慢者，捉二人後到，罰壹角；全不來者，罰半瓮。眾團破除。
  - 一、或有大人顛言到(倒)儀，罰醴膩一筵；小人不聽上人，罰羯羊壹口，酒壹瓮。
  - 一、或有凶事榮親者，告保(報)錄事，行文放帖，各自兢兢，一一指實，記錄人名目。
  - **Members:** 錄事孔闍梨、虞候安闍梨、社人連真、社人恩子、社人福子、社人吳家女、社人連保、社人富連、社人勝子、社人員泰、社人子富、社人員意.
  - **Closing:** 右入社條件，在後不承文帖及出社者，罰醴膩一筵。
- **My translation.**
  - "On the 24th day of the first month of the wuchen year, the women of the [䄠]-ward lane, sitting together, agreed and set these rules. The whole society settled them."
  - **Births and deaths:** "For births and deaths that each [member's household] arranges, [each] pays one *dou* of flour, all alike, without slackness. The last two latecomers are fined one *jiao* [of wine]; anyone absent altogether, half a jar. The group consumes it."
  - **Disorder:** "If an adult speaks or behaves out of order (顛言倒儀), she is fined a feast. If a junior does not obey her seniors, she is fined **a castrated ram and a jar of wine**."
  - **Funerals:** "When there is a funeral for a kinsperson, report to the Secretary, who issues the circular. Each [member] is to be diligent and confirm one by one, and the names are recorded."
  - **Close:** "These are the terms of joining. Whoever afterwards ignores a circular or leaves the society is fined a feast."
- **Editors' notes.**
  - The first character of the place-name (礻+圭) is read 雇 by Naba and 旌 by Wang Zhongmin. The editors keep the graph (p. 28 n. 1).
  - Dating: the cyclical date puts it in the Guiyijun period. S.527 also lists a 社人富連. "如果這兩個富連是同一人", the wuchen year is 968, "but this is a single piece of evidence and hard to settle" (p. 28).
- **Who the officers were.** Both named officers carry the clerical title **闍梨** (ācārya, "teacher"): the Secretary 孔闍梨 and the *yuhou* 安闍梨. In a women's she these are most likely nuns. This is my inference; the editors do not comment.
  - Clergy as she officers is not unusual. The men's 兄弟 circular S.6981 (doc. 35) is headed by 社官闍梨 and a 小闍梨 (p. 101).
- **No officer called 社長 or 社官 is listed.** The two officers are a Secretary and a *yuhou* (an enforcement officer).

**(3) P.4635, 公元九四五年前後（?）社家女人便麵油曆 (doc. 246, pp. 484–486).** A loan ledger of the "women of the she households".
- **The entries.** Dated the 7th of a lost month, the ledger lists about thirty women borrowing flour (in *cheng* 秤 and *jin*) and oil (in *ping* 瓶), each to repay at autumn with **about 50% added**:
  - "瑩保孃便麵壹秤，至秋壹秤半" ("Yingbao's mother borrows one *cheng* of flour; at autumn, one and a half");
  - "穆家女便麵貳斤，至秋叁斤";
  - "董婆便麵叁斤，至秋肆斤半";
  - "曹家保瑩便油叁瓶子，至秋肆瓶子半".
- **Who borrowed.** Borrowers are named by kin role: "X's mother" (孃, 母), "wife of" (妻), "daughter of the family" (家女), "old woman" (婆).
- **Records of repayment and surety.**
  - Glosses record part-payments: 齊家瑩了 borrowed six *cheng*, owed nine, and the gloss says "納得一秤，欠八秤" ("paid one, owes eight").
  - One entry names a **guarantor**, 口承人齊粉塠 (p. 485).
- **The editors' reading** (說明, p. 486). The ledger dates to about 945, because 張賢住 appears in a Jingtu Monastery account of 945 (P.2032v). Their gloss: "敦煌的一些社邑置有自己的公共積累——『義聚』，以備急難。『義聚』中的物品也用來貸給社人，本曆就是**社人的家屬**從『義聚』中借貸麵油的實例" ("some she kept a common store, the *yiju*, against emergencies, and lent from it to members. This ledger is a case of **members' family members** borrowing flour and oil from the *yiju*").
  - So the editors do *not* call this a women's she. They read it as the women of member households borrowing from an ordinary she's store.

**(4) Laywomen's associations before 867 (appendix).**
- 開皇三年（583）十月八日優婆夷邑造經題記 (Fujii Yūrinkan). The colophon is only "優婆夷邑敬造供養" ("the *upāsikā* association reverently made this offering"), from Ikeda's colophon catalogue (p. 750).
- 天寶十二載（753）五月廿三日優婆夷社寫經題記 (Tianjin Museum 27): "優婆夷社寫" ("copied by the *upāsikā* she") (p. 756).
- The 594 association of 邑人淨通師 lists many women among its members, such as 封雲娥, 賈叔女, 樂貴娥 and 張元妃 (P.2086, p. 751). It is a mixed *yi*.

**(5) Other women in she documents** (from the table of contents, not read in full).
- Death circulars and gift ledgers for members' mothers, wives, a 阿婆 and 阿姊師 (戊子年 988 安定阿姊師身亡轉帖, doc. 43).
- Sacrificial texts by she for women: 祭蘇氏文 (824), 祭似氏文, 祭曹氏文 (docs. 318, 321, 323).
- S.6005 (doc. 6) shows the "three loads" (三馱) system. Each member could register household dependants for funeral claims; its list notes "阿姑請一贈" and "阿婆三馱了" (p. 22).

### The model bylaws that the women copied (S.6537v)

- **Purpose:** "凡論邑義，濟苦救貧" ("the point of an association is to relieve the suffering and rescue the poor") (p. 51).
- **Officers chosen by age and ability** (fifteen-member model, p. 50). "老者請為社長，次者充為社官，但是事當其理，……請為錄事" ("the eldest is asked to be President, the next serves as Manager, and whoever handles affairs properly is asked to be Secretary").
- **Membership passes to sons and daughters, not to collaterals** (p. 52): "本身若也盡終，便須男女承受……至有闔門無人，不許遺他枝眷" ("when the member dies, her/his **sons and daughters** must take it over … if the whole house is extinct, it may not be passed to other branches"). This corrects Gernet's "sons and grandsons" (p. 268) to *男女*.
- **Weddings as well as funerals:** "更有營就，男女人事，合行共成" ("for weddings, men's and women's affairs, all act together") (p. 51).
- **Penalties:**
  - a drunken brawler at the feast: "大者罰醴膩一席，少者決杖十三" ("an elder pays a feast; a junior, thirteen strokes") (p. 52);
  - a member who is "拗戾無端" ("perverse without cause") is expelled: "便任逐出社內" (p. 52).

## 3. Design table

| # | Current design | Pressure | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | House common store (Gernet note: `tm_house_changzhu`; Rong note: 長生). | The she's own word for its common store is **義聚 *yiju*, "the righteous store"**, held "以備急難" ("against emergencies") and lent to members (pp. 15, 486). | Name the House store **義聚** in Chinese loc for Yutai Houses ("the righteous store"). Keep 長生 for a *guan*'s endowment if one is founded (Rong row 2). Two stores: the House's (sisters' dues) and the temple's (consecrated). |
| 2 | Gernet row 5: lend to sisters at no interest. | **Contradicted for she.** P.4635 shows women borrowing from the she store at about 50% to autumn, with guarantors and part-payment glosses. Interest-free lending was a *monastery-to-its-own-serfs* practice. | Revise: House loans to sisters' households carry the harvest increase (repay ×1.5) and need a guarantor sister. Default creates strain, not violence. The gain goes to the store, not to a creditor. Loc: "Borrow a *cheng*, return a *cheng* and a half at autumn; Sister Fendui stands surety." |
| 3 | Burial dues on a member's death (S.527). | P.3489 levies dues for **births and deaths** alike (營生死, one *dou* of flour each). The fifteen-member model adds **weddings** (男女人事). | Extend the dues mechanic: a sister's **completed gestation (month 10 birth)** and **Declare Before Witnesses** trigger the same dues as a death, and the House holds a feast. This gives the Jade Embryo's birth a social reality. Loc: "For every birth and every death in the House, a *dou* of flour from each sister, all alike." |
| 4 | Take as Daughter; adoption keeps the rites. | The model bylaw passes membership to **sons and daughters (男女承受)** and **forbids** passing it to collateral branches when a house dies out (p. 52). | Mechanic: a sister's House seat passes to her (taken or fostered) daughter on death. Without a daughter the seat lapses. That makes Take as Daughter the way to keep a seat. Loc: "No niece may take the seat; only a daughter." |
| 5 | Elder Sister on a term by lot; nüguan as Manager (S.527 note). | P.3489's officers are a **Secretary and a *yuhou*, both titled 闍梨** (probably nuns). The model chooses officers by **age** (eldest = President) and **ability** (Secretary). | Keep the lot, but draw it among the three eldest nominees. Add the *yuhou* (虞候, "the warden") as a House flavour title who enforces fines. A nüguan may hold Secretary or warden; never assume she is the head. |
| 6 | Discipline: brawl at the feast, leaving costs a feast and strokes (S.527 note row 5). | The women's she cut the model's **thirty** strokes for leaving to **three**. P.3489 drops the stick altogether: a feast for leaving, and **a castrated ram and a jar of wine** for a junior who defies her seniors. | Confirms the soft penalty: leaving a House costs gold (a feast), never a wound. Add a "the junior's defiance" event: she pays a ram and wine (gold), seniors gain opinion, and strain falls. |
| 7 | House name: none ("named by its list", Galambos). | P.3489 names the she by **ward and lane** (坊巷女人) and calls it a **團** (group) sitting together. | Dynamic House name: "the women of [county capital]'s lane". This beats "House of Elder Sister X" when there is no Elder Sister. |

## 4. In-world loc lines

- "The women of the lane sat together and set these rules; the whole House agreed them."
- "For every birth and every death, one *dou* of flour from each sister, all alike. The last two to arrive pay a cup; the absent, half a jar."
- "A grown woman who speaks out of turn pays a feast. A junior who will not hear her elders pays a ram and a jar of wine."
- "Borrow a *cheng* of flour in spring, return a *cheng* and a half at autumn. Sister Fendui stands surety."
- "When a sister dies, her daughter takes her seat. No niece, no cousin: a daughter, or the seat stays empty."
- "The brothers wrote 'the elder as elder brothers'. We wrote sisters."
