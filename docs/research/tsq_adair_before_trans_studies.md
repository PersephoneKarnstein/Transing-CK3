## 1. Argument in brief

A reply to Andrea Long Chu and Emmett Harsin Drager's "After Trans Studies" (TSQ 6.1, 2019), which called the field a "church" of pieties and asked for fiercer argument. The authors answer that "trans studies can't be 'over' because, in fact, it isn't yet here" (abstract, p. 306). The field's real limit is money and jobs: "the 'great failure' of trans studies is that we can't all afford to write" (p. 309). They cite a materialist line (Namaste, Salah, Prosser, Bettcher) centred on sex workers and poor transsexuals (pp. 310–311), and list "lost" trans scholars who left or were never cited (pp. 312–313). Against the field as a battlefield they offer cultivation and care: "why would we want our small corner of the small corner of/adjacent to the university to be a battleground rather than a shelter?" (p. 312). They warn that care work can become a "revolving door" that uses people up (p. 315).
