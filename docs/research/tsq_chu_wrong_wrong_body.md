# Chu, "The Wrong Wrong Body" (2017): trans phenomenology, design reading

**Citation (checked against the article text).** Andrea Long Chu, "The Wrong Wrong Body: Notes on Trans Phenomenology," book review, *TSQ: Transgender Studies Quarterly* 4, no. 1 (February 2017): 141–152. DOI 10.1215/23289252-3711613. Duke University Press.
Books reviewed: Juliet Jacques, *Trans: A Memoir* (Verso, 2015); Gayle Salamon, *Assuming a Body* (Columbia, 2010); Jay Prosser, *Second Skins* (Columbia, 1998).
Read in full, with the note and references, from a text extraction kept outside the repo. Page numbers are the journal's.

**Relation to existing notes.** `book_bhatt_chu.md` part II reads Chu's later book *Females* (tag **CH** in beliefs.md). This is an earlier, short review. It is theory, with no historical content. The note is short to match.

## 1. The argument in brief

- **Jacques makes transition boring.** Chu calls *Trans* "the first transition memoir to have succeeded in making transition boring" (p. 142). It is about temp jobs, depression and the NHS. Jacques calls transition "a bunch of hoops to jump through while working in boring jobs" (p. 142).
- **Transition as waiting.** "Trans is a study in waiting" (p. 143): for hormones, for editors, for surgery. Its mood is going nowhere slowly, not a hero's journey (p. 143).
- **The wrong society.** Jacques felt "not trapped in the wrong body but trapped in the wrong society" (p. 143, quoting Jacques). Surgery does not fix rent or work (p. 143).
- **Against spectacle, not essence.** Jacques objects to how the wrong-body story is sensationalised, from Jorgensen to Jenner. Whether it is "essentialist" does not interest her (p. 144). In such a society "the only right kind of wrong body is the sensationalized wrong body" (p. 144).
- **Salamon.** She says the felt sense of the body is real but socially built. Chu says this turns phenomenology into a "strategic" essentialism. Phenomenology starts from what appears, not from what lies behind it (pp. 145–147).
- **Prosser.** He takes the trans felt sense at its word: wrong embodiment "is simply what transsexuality feels like" (p. 148, quoting Prosser). But he puts the "literal" body in the future, after surgery. He tends to make surgery "the definitive transsexual experience" (p. 149).
- **Chu's turn.** The real, present body is the wrong body itself. "the wrong body is literally, nakedly wrong" (p. 149). But only that body can feel its wrongness. Chu calls the trans felt sense a "nonce ontology": a sincere, improvised account of being, made in the middle of life (pp. 149–150).
- **Livable.** To call transition barely livable "must already be to say that transition is livable" (p. 150). The book is about "getting a body as good as you can get it" (p. 151).

## 2. What is usable for the mod

No period content. Its use is for **tone and for one doctrine question**.

1. **The knife is not the whole of it.** Chu's critique of Prosser is a critique of surgery as the one true threshold. The Adelphai rite risks the same thing: "the cut are women" can read as "the uncut are not yet women".
2. **Write the waiting.** Several faiths already wait. The Day of Blood comes once a year. The Dorley programme lasts three years. The Turning waits for Adar. Chu and Jacques say the wait is the experience. It is ordinary, slow and dull.
3. **The wrong society.** Jacques's line fits the materialist strand (TMx, Bhatt): the trouble is the world, not the self. It also fits beliefs A4, "transness needs no cause".
4. **No spectacle.** The mod's rename and re-embodiment events should not be reveal scenes. Avoid before-and-after framing.

## 4. Cautions

- **Not a case against surgery.** Chu does not argue that surgery is wrong or optional for everyone. She argues against making it the single meaning of transition. Do not use this note to weaken the knife or the other faiths' bodily gates.
- **"Nonce ontology" is a theory term.** Do not put it, or Heidegger, into loc.
- **Modern context.** The NHS, Verso and David Cameron are 2010s Britain. Use the shape of the argument, not its scenery.
- **Attribution.** "Trapped in the wrong society" is Jacques's line, quoted by Chu. Attribute it to Jacques in docs. In loc, paraphrase it in-world.
