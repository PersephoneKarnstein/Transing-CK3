# Bhatt, *Trans/Rad/Fem* (2025): "The Third Sex" and "Degendering and Regendering", design reading

Source: Talia Bhatt, *Trans/Rad/Fem: Essays on Transfeminism, Book 1* (epub, same text as `book_bhatt_chu.md`).
Chapters read in full for this pass: "The Third Sex" (epub 23), "Degendering and Regendering" (epub 16),
third-sex passages of "When the Doll Speaks" (epub 19). Quotes verbatim. This note supersedes the one-row
summary of "The Third Sex" in `book_bhatt_chu.md` for design purposes.

## 1. The argument in brief

- **The binary is a hierarchy, not a pair.** "'The Gender Binary' is a misnomer; gender has always been a hierarchy." The chapter's opening parable: a machine sorting Ones and Zeroes quietly files its NULL exceptions under Zero.
- **Third-sexing is how patriarchy works, not how it is resisted.** "The existence of a third sex does no more to challenge societal male-supremacy than does the existence of a fourth, fifth, or even second sex. Every sex that is not the First Amongst Sexes ... simply becomes another sexual resource to be exploited." And: "Third-Sexing, far from being a challenge to patriarchy, seems to be a surprisingly historical feature of its operation."
- **The third sex is a dumping-ground.** "'hijra' is an expansive category, one that is meant to encompass all those deemed—bluntly—sexually 'defective'. Girls who do not menstruate may be considered hijra ... The 'third sex', such as it is, is not a prescriptive category, but a dumping-ground." The same logic hits barren women (*baanjh*), widows, women who bear only daughters, old women.
- **Human, broodmare, or freak.** "the heterosexual regime has always regarded people as one of human, broodmare, or freak. If you are not a person with autonomy, then you are a vessel for those who are ... and if you cannot even be that, then you are a waste of flesh."
- **"Veneration" is marginalisation.** A record of hijras "being prescribed a livelihood of begging for alms at ceremonies, is not 'reverence' or an 'institutionalized gender-role', but marginalization." "You do not want the 'veneration' that the holy men of my culture reserve for us."
- **No golden age.** "There is no salvation awaiting us in a glorified past that does not exist." Quoting Bubbles Khanum: "the over glorification of the past does more harm than good."
- **Against ranking androgyny above bodily transition.** Nanda elevates the "transgenderist" over the "transsexual", "pedestalizing a pure gender 'disruption' untainted by medical technologies"; Bhatt calls the idea that transition is regressive "facile, absurd ... actively eliminationist." Hijras take hormones by the handful; Revathi: "I consider myself a woman. But I possessed the form of a man. I wanted to rid myself of that form."
- **The barren-contamination taboo shows hijras are read as barren women.** Brides are kept from hijras lest their infertility spread, a belief "not one that is usually applied to men."
- **Respectability is betrayal.** Affluent trans women "seek[ing] to distance themselves from the abjectified hijra and advocate for themselves as a more respectable breed of queer. Their treasonous politics will not soon be forgiven."
- **Decolonial romanticism is attacked directly.** She rejects Gill-Peterson's "hijras were not then—and are not today—transgender" as decolonial-feminist idealisation, and the NALSA judgment's third-sexing of hijras as Western anthropology turned into Indian law.
- **Degendering vs regendering (epub 16).** Trans women are degendered into a "third thing"; trans men are regendered as "lapsed reproductive assets." The regime sees all trans people as "mules who mutilated ourselves into sterility."

## 2. What it changes in the design

| # | Current design | Bhatt's pressure | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dorleyism framed as a **preservationist** sect keeping the 867 eunuch-angel theology alive (beliefs.md G2 consequence 7). | "Veneration" of eunuchs was a cage; no golden past. Historians' "three genders of Byzantium" is the academic third-sexing she attacks. | Recast: the Sisters do **not** preserve the third estate. They take one thing from the court (the cut are not men) and refuse the rest (that they are a third thing, or angels, or property). Era-decay events show the court's "reverence" and its slave market as one system. |
| 2 | Galenic splinter ("castrates are a third kind") planned as a neutral alternative rite. | Third-sexing is the regime's own move. | Keep the Galenic rite, but write it as the **accommodationist** wing: the respectable bargain with the eunuch estate. Its tradeoffs and flavour should make that visible. The Aristotelian main rite is the faith's position. |
| 3 | Jade Embryo polemic "we are not eunuchs"; the knife "the eunuch's road, and the Way does not walk it". Grounded in Chiang. | Read against Bhatt this risks the respectability politics she calls treason: distancing from the abject castrated class. Chiang's caution against calling eunuchs "transgender" is close to the Gill-Peterson position she rejects. | Keep the gate (inner gestation, a full bodily transition, which Bhatt supports). Re-aim the polemic at the **palace's classification**, not at eunuchs: "the palace calls us eunuchs so it need not call us women." Resolve the open schism question in Bhatt's direction: a eunuch who seeks womanhood is a sister, and the Way receives her. |
| 4 | Alms and begging as a period-true clergy trait (H21). | Alms-begging is marginalisation recorded as reverence. | Keep the doctrine, but flavour it as mutual aid inside the order, never as a sacred social role granted by outsiders. |
| 5 | Sisterhood centred on Marked and daughters. | The dumping-ground takes barren women, widows, girls who never bleed. "If you are not of the First Sex ... Third-Sexing will come for you sooner or later." | New mechanic candidate: the refuge and arrivals also take in **barren and widowed women** cast out by the reproductive order; they can be taken as daughters and count as sisters. Fits Take as Daughter and the barren Mother (H20). |
| 6 | Infertility of daughters as a rule (H40). | The regime's insult is "mules ... into sterility." | No mechanical change. Write the faith's theology so barrenness is no defect (the Mother is barren), and give hostile faiths the "mule" and contamination slurs as reaction text. |
| 7 | Re-embodiment changes sex, not only gender. | Bhatt (and her Raymond chapter) insists trans women change sex, not just gender. | Confirms the design. No change. |
| 8 | Hostile reaction text. | The bride taboo; "human, broodmare, or freak"; "a parody of femininity". | Use as vanilla-faith reaction text and wedding events: hostile courts keep daughters away from brides. |
