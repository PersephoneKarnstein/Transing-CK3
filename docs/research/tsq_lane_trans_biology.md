## 1. The argument in brief

- **Feminism's habit.** Feminists rightly distrust biology, since it was used to keep women out. But some have treated any mention of the body as essentialist and left the field to biologists (p. 185).
- **Method is not theory.** Social constructionism is a method. It does not require a socialisation theory of how gender develops (pp. 185–186).
- **Both sides have been weapons.** "Both biological determinist and socialization theories of gender have been mobilized for regressive and progressive political and social purposes" (p. 186). Nazi biology was used against gay and trans people. Socialisation theory was used for reparative therapy (p. 186).
- **Study up.** Lane studies the researchers and their power, not trans people (p. 187).
- **Nested dichotomies.** Female/male, pathological/normal, trans/cis, transgender/transsexual, subverting/maintaining (p. 188). Each leads research into "blind alleys" (p. 188).
- **The religious version.** For conservative religion and determinism, "all exceptions are monstrous errors that go against God's law or nature's design" (p. 188).
- **Two activist camps.** One treats transsexualism as "an immutable neurological intersex condition" and transgender as "social gender play". The other praises gender subversion and calls transsexuals conformist (p. 188). Lane rejects both.
- **The proposal.** Use newer biology: nonlinear, contingent, open-ended. Read the research as showing "a drive to alter the sexed body that has some neurological correlates—which are not dichotomous but diverse" (p. 189). Treat gender variance "as a healthy part of human variation, not as pathology or disorder" (p. 189).

## 2. What is usable for the mod

1. **The Marked trait is a born trait.** `tm_transgender` is random at birth and `genetic = yes` (the user's v1 call; faith_design open question 4). That is a "born this way" model. Lane's view allows it: there may be a bodily drive, but it is not a binary and not destiny.
2. **The hostile voices can take both sides.** Lane's point that nature and nurture are both used as weapons (p. 186) maps onto period hostility:
   - *Nature's error.* Otto of Freising's resurrection "corrected"; the surgeons' "nature erred" (DeVun §2.2).
   - *Bad upbringing or corruption.* Peter of Abano treats some passivity as innate (Karras E169). A preacher blames the eunuchs' houses or the Sisters' "contagion" (Serano §3.6).
   Hostile text gains realism if it uses both.
3. **A4 already holds Lane's line.** "Transness needs no cause; asking why is idle." Lane would add: you can ask, but no answer should sort people into real and fake.
4. **Lane's two camps mirror the Adelphai schism.** "Immutable condition" against "gender play" is close to the main rite against the Galenic wing. The mod already follows Bhatt and does not treat the Galenic wing as a neutral alternative.

## 4. Cautions

- **Intersex conflation.** The "neurological intersex condition" camp (p. 188) folds trans women into intersex. The mod must not do this. Do not use the *khuntha* (Sanders, Almarri) or Hildegard's bodies (Libby) as an explanation for the Marked.
- **Terms.** Lane uses "transgender" for identities that break the male/female categories, "transsexual" for crossing, and "trans" for both (p. 190, note 1). The mod's daughters cross. Do not borrow Lane's "transgender" as a third-category label.
- **No period science.** Lane writes about modern neuroscience. Any in-world cause theory must use period terms: humours, seed, heat, the stars.
- **Dated debate.** The essay is from 2016, before the turn to materialist transfeminism the mod follows. Use it only for the both-sides point and the healthy-variation framing.
