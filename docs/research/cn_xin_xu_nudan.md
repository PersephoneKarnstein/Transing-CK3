## 1. The argument

- **Thesis.** Nüdan presents a "gender-symmetrical, pre-sexual symbolic culture". Both sexes revert "the sexualized, adult bodies into the unsexualized bodies of the girl and boy" in order to gestate the inner elixir (abstract, p. 1). The method is "not … advancing forward, but … stepping back".
- **Definition of "pre-sexual"** (p. 2). The regression of secondary sex characteristics "to recover a child-like physique". "Because pre-sexual bodies retain primary sex characteristics, this concept remains distinct from … 'genderless' or 'de-gendered'." **On this reading the nüdan adept ends with a girl's body, not a man's and not a sexless one.**
- **Explicitly not transition** (p. 16): "The movement described in the texts is not a transition from one gender to another, nor a fusion of the two sexes into a hybrid form, but a return from gender to the undifferentiated One." It is "subtractive", not additive.
- **Dating.** Following Valussi, nüdan became a category only in the 17th–18th centuries. That move also "retrofit[ted]" earlier neidan as "Male Alchemy (nandan 男丹)" (p. 2). Most nüdan scriptures, including those ascribed to Xi Wangmu, He Xiangu and Sun Bu'er, were composed in the Qing by Min Yide, Fu Jinquan and Liu Yiming (p. 3; n. 1).
  - Xiao Dengfu's claim of earlier roots is reported but deliberately excluded (pp. 2–3). He says the *Taixuan baodian* 太玄寶典 (late Tang) has women focus on blood, and that Xue Daoguang 薛道光 (Southern Song) gave a breast-circulation method in his *Wuzhen pian* commentary. These are the only possible in-period anchors for women's practice, and they come through a secondary source. Verify before use.
- **The textual variant on the end state** (p. 14). Yan Zehuan's *Nannü dangong yitong bian* (1903) reads, for both sexes: "his body returns to that of a child … her body returns to that of a child" (男子白虎降則變為童體 … 女子赤龍斬則變為童體). Liu Yiming's 1798 text as printed by Valussi has 男體 ("male body") for the woman. The authors read "child" as the meaning.
- **Valussi's critical view, reported** (p. 4). Some texts call the finished adept *nüzhong zhangfu* or "man", "thus indicating an inherent deficiency" (Valussi 2014). Also: "A man can ascend [to Heaven] on his own; a woman, instead, needs to await salvation" (*Nüdan hebian*, via Valussi 2010, p. 16). The authors concede "problematic expressions" (p. 16).
- **Period texts quoted (usable in 867–1453).**
  - *Qingjing jing* 清靜經 (Tang or later; n. 6): "The male is clear and the female is turbid, the male is active and the female is still" (男清女濁，男動女靜, p. 7).
  - Li Zhenren (Song): "Having achieved Pure yang, one is then called the Great yin" (既成純陽，則是謂太陰, p. 6).
  - Chen Zhixu 陳致虛 (1335): "To conform is to be mortal, to revert is to be immortal" (順之則凡，逆之則仙, p. 6).
- **Symmetry of the two paths.**
  - Each body holds the other's seed (Xue Xinxiang, 1825). Man is Li ☲, yang outside and yin inside; woman is Kan ☵, yin outside and yang inside. "This is but a borrowed vessel" (此寄體也, p. 7).
  - After a man "tames the White Tiger … his penis will shrink, resembling that of a child" (p. 9).
  - After a woman cuts the dragon, "her breasts will shrink, back to a prepubescent state" (p. 9).
- **Breast refinement.** *Da nü jindan jue* 大女金丹訣 (before 1834): rub "seventy-two times each, first gently then urgently … In one hundred days, breasts can become like two walnuts" (p. 12). The menses fade "from red to yellow, from yellow to white, from white to nothingness, and from nothingness to qi" (*Kunyuan jing*, p. 13). The breath is called *yuru* 玉乳, "jade milk" (p. 13).
- **Images of the embryo stirring.** "A woman's *yuye* [jade liquid] is the marrow of White Phoenix, refined from her Red Dragon" (赤龍化為白鳳之髓). When it forms, "it is like a child bumping the *pin*; … like the top of a half-cooked egg pushing up; … a clanging sound" (p. 14). Also: "It is not a real fetus; rather, it is where the breath of *shen* resides" (*Nü jindan*, p. 14).
- **Men become pregnant too** (p. 15). Neidan uses "Embryonic Immortal" (*taixian* 胎仙), "Suckling" (*buru* 哺乳) and "Fetus Coming Out" (*chutai* 出胎). "Male cultivation must pass through a 'feminine' form, not because men become women, but because the capacity to gestate is the sign of returning to origin" (p. 15). **This is the closest any source in the batch comes to a male-to-female movement, and the authors close it off.**
- **Desire as potency.** Yue Fujia (1635) on the hour of desire: "reverse the process and seize this moment [and you] form the *dan*; follow the natural course … and [you] form a fetus" (逆而取之則成丹，順而施之則成胎, p. 11).
- **Citation flag.** The authors cite "Valussi 2022, p. 451" for the retrofitting of nandan. That cannot be the Ca' Foscari chapter (pp. 205–220); it is presumably another 2022 Valussi publication. Check before citing.

## 3. In-world lines

- Faith (from Li Zhenren, Song; unattributed in-game): "Their pure yang, when it is finished, they call great yin. We only begin where they end."
- Faith (after Chen Zhixu, 1335): "To follow is to die; to turn back is to live. We turn back, and keep going."
- Hostile scholar-official (*Qingjing jing*): "The male is clear and the female is turbid. You would muddy clear water and call it a rite."
- Faith answer: "Still water clears. Ask your own scripture."
- Month 6 (draft): "The white tiger has lain down, and I have not gone back to being a boy."
- Month 10 (draft): "Something pushes at the crown like the top of an egg; a sound like a struck bell; then I am here."
