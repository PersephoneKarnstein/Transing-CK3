# Japan faith: lexicon check (2026-10-25)

Sources: **OZ** = 宮腰・石井・小田編『旺文社 図解全訳古語辞典』(2021), read from page images (printed page numbers given). **ONCOJ** = Oxford-NINJAL Corpus of Old Japanese dictionary (Nara-period lemmas; an attestation there only shows a word is old). Quotes are short; translations mine. Images deleted after reading.

Note what OZ is: a school dictionary of about 25k headwords. "No headword" means only that this dictionary has no entry, not that the word is unattested. Where a word is missing, the table says where it does turn up.

## 2. Answers to the nine questions

1. **あね.** OZ has no *ane* entry and no sense of address. Its only evidence (note under いもうと) is that Heian women used *ane* or *konokami* for an elder sister, so a woman calling another woman *ane* is in period. Calling a non-kin sponsor *ane* is the mod's own extension (already marked invented). The attested words for a senior woman: **このかみ** (③ "the leading figure in a group", 源氏・若菜下); **刀自** (② honorific for old or senior women, 平家); **御許** (address to a woman, but implies service). **大君** (eldest daughter of a noble) and **上﨟** (court rank; later "courtesan") do not fit.
2. **長者.** Yes. Sense ③: 「駅家の長。駅長。また、駅長を兼ねた遊女屋の主人」("head of a post station; also the master of an *asobi* house who doubled as station head"). No citation is given. The head is not gendered here; Goodwin's sources give the women heads.
3. **なづけ.** Not in OZ. Its neighbour なつく(下二) gives なつけ "to tame, to win over" (源氏・若紫), an unlucky near-homophone for this scene. Better: **あざな** ② "the name others habitually call one by". Avoid **なのり** (a boy's coming-of-age name).
4. **唐櫃.** Fits: OZ says it holds 衣装 (clothing). In 1178 it was also read *karauto/karato*. *Karabitsu* is still correct, and players know it better.
5. **うけつぐ.** Not a headword. The idea is attested as the gloss of 受く③ (源氏・若菜下). Treat *uketsugi* as unverified for 1178, as the design already does.
6. **刀自 vs 長者.** Keep ***chōja*** as the office: it is the documented title, and OZ links it to the *asobi* house. 刀自 means "mistress of a household" (and 家刀自 "housewife"), which would make the house domestic. But 刀自 sense ② and 平家's 母刀自 make ***toji*** a good **honorific or address** for the house's older women: "Mother" → *haha-toji* or plain *toji*.
7. **今様.** Confirmed: ① "modern style, fashion of the day"; 今様歌 "sung by shirabyōshi and *asobi*", in *Ryōjin hishō*. *Imayō no Ie* reads as "the houses of the present-day song" and also "the houses in the modern style" (a harmless double sense).
8. **遊女.** Use ***asobi***: OZ's sense ③ with the 更級日記 citation (c. 1060). *Yūjo* has no entry and is the Sino-Japanese gloss; *asobi-me* is the long form; *ukareme* ("drifting woman") is listed as a synonym without its own entry and is best left out. OZ's note on the trade: "a woman who entertained with song, dance and music"; nothing more explicit.
9. **家 vs 宿.** Keep **家 *ie***: senses "household / family members" and "lineage" both fit an adoptive house with a song line. **宿 *yado*** means home, inn and (⑤) "husband", and 方丈記's 「舞人を宿せる仮屋」 shows it for lodging performers. Good in event prose about the lodging trade, wrong for a faith name.

## 3. Recommendations for the design

- **Faith name: *Imayō no Ie*.** Keep. Both words check out.
- **Daughter trait: *Ie no Onna*.** Keep. It is plain period vocabulary (家 + 女). One caution: OZ's 家刀自 "mistress of a household" sits close by. Write the tooltip as "woman of the house" = a woman counted among the house's women, not the house's matron.
- **House head: *chōja*.** Keep (OZ ③ + Goodwin). In dialogue, address her as ***toji*** / ***haha-toji*** (平家 母刀自), or English "Mother".
- **Second office, elder sister: *ane*.** Keep, glossed "the house's elder sister", invented office. OZ supports *ane* as a women's word. Optional flavour: in the office description, "she is the house's *konokami*, the one who stands over the younger women" (源氏). Don't make *konokami* the title: its first sense is "eldest son".
- **Address between women:** *omoto* ("you", to a woman, 源氏) in dialogue. Never *imo/imōto* (a man's word; ONCOJ "girl").
- **Naming:** replace *nazuke* with ***azana*** ("the name the house calls her by", OZ ②), or keep English "the naming". Never *nanori*.
- **Line receiving:** keep *uketsugi* only with the README's "invented use" note; English "the receiving of the line" in loc.
- **Robe-chest:** *karabitsu*, once, glossed "the chest for her robes". OK.
- **Women of the trade:** *asobi* everywhere. No *yūjo*, *jorō* or *ukareme*.
- **Aohaka *kugutsu*:** name them, but never gloss *kugutsu* as "puppet". That runs into the design's no-doll rule. Prefer "the singers of Aohaka" in loc.
- **Clergy *utame*** (design §2): no OZ headword. Still unverified; fall back to "Mother of the Song".
- All recommended words are ASCII plus ō: *chōja, toji, haha-toji, ane, azana, omoto, karabitsu, asobi, Imayō no Ie, Ie no Onna*.
