# DiPietro, "Of Huachafería, Así, and M'e Mati" (TSQ 2016): design reading

**Citation.** Pedro Javier DiPietro, "Of Huachafería, Así, and M'e Mati: Decolonizing Transing Methodologies," *TSQ: Transgender Studies Quarterly* 3, nos. 1–2 (May 2016): 65–73. DOI 10.1215/23289252-3334211.

**What was read.** The whole essay, notes and references included. Page numbers are the journal's.

**Verdict in one line.** A short methods essay in the Lugones line. Nothing in it is usable for the design.

---

## 1. Relation to existing notes

**It sits squarely in the literature Bhatt attacks.** It opens from Lugones: women of color share "histories of being rendered genderless as not-women" (p. 65). It builds on Gill-Peterson's 2014 TSQ essay (p. 66), on Wynter, and on Mignolo's "epistemic disobedience". Bhatt's ch. 5 is an attack on Lugones's "coloniality of gender" and its sources (`book_bhatt_brown_trans_les.md` §2.6). The essay's closing contrast of non-Western "doing rather than being, connectivity rather than individuality" with Western either/or thinking (p. 70) is the kind of West/non-West split Bhatt calls a "preserved, primitive 'living past'" (§2.5).

**It does not make the golden-age claim directly.** Its argument is about translation and method, not about a precolonial past.

## 2. Argument in brief

- Trans and queer studies take the human body as a neutral "zero degree"; women-of-color thought starts from bodies made into non-people (pp. 65–66).
- Three examples of "transing methodologies" (p. 66):
  - Cornejo's essay on a Lima trans child (TSQ 1.3, 2014) fails: it translates the child's Limeño Spanish and slang into English queer theory, so "a queer touch may be revealed as colonizing violence" (pp. 66–68, 70).
  - An Argentine travesti activist describes her becoming as wanting to be "like that" (*así*) the first time she met other travestis: becoming among others, not imitation of femininity (p. 69).
  - Wekker's Surinamese informant corrects a question about identity: not "I am a mati" but "I mati", a verb of doing (pp. 69–70).
- Conclusion: dwell in "the opacity of non-Western distinctions of the real" (p. 70).

## 3. What is usable

Nothing for mechanics or lore.

The *así* passage (p. 69) describes something general: a girl sees women like herself for the first time and knows she will be "like that". The mod's House intake already carries this. If a flavour line is ever wanted for a seeker's first meeting with a sister, write it fresh. It needs no source, and none of the essay's terms belong in the mod.

## 4. What it changes in the design

Nothing. No table.

## 5. Cautions

- *Travesti*, *mati*, *huachafería* and *lunfardo* terms belong to living communities and modern slang. Keep them out of the mod.
- Winti religion and Aymara and Quechua worldviews are named only in passing (p. 70). The essay gives no evidence about them; do not cite it for them.
- If the essay is cited at all, cite it as an example of the decolonial method Bhatt rejects.
