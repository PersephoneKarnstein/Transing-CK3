# The Dorley precedent — penal castration as rehabilitation

User direction (2026-10-14): lean **yes** on penal castration, but lampshade it as "the Dorley precedent" with an event, or let the player choose early in the campaign. This note summarises *The Sisters of Dorley* (Alyson Greaves) and proposes the in-game mapping.

## Plot outline

*Welcome to Dorley Hall* (2022; book 1 of *The Sisters of Dorley*, published serially on AO3/Scribblehub/Patreon; a print edition exists, a second volume is in launch as *Secrets of Dorley Hall*, Nov 2026).

Stefan Riley's older-brother figure Mark Vogel vanishes from the Royal College of Saint Almsworth. A year later Stefan meets a woman who could be Mark's twin and learns that dozens of "troubled or unruly" young men have disappeared from the college over the years, while a handful of look-alike women live in Dorley Hall, a women's dormitory. Stefan, a closeted trans girl, gets himself admitted to the college and deliberately provokes capture so that whatever happened to Mark will happen to her. She wakes in a cell in the Hall's hidden second basement alongside an intake of young men, is assigned a sponsor (Pippa), and goes through the first year of the programme while Christine, a near-graduate who sees through her, helps from above. Melissa (Mark) returns; the book follows Stefan/Stephanie's transition, the intake's conflicts (Aaron, Adam, Will, Declan, Ollie, Raph, Martin), one washout (Declan), and the sponsors' and Aunt Bea's defence of the whole enterprise.

## The programme (as the wiki records it)

**Two regimes.**
- *Grandmother's Dorley (1962–2004).* Dorothy Marsden, after a kidnapping and castration that ended in two deaths, was blackmailed by Crispin Smyth-Farrow into running an extortion, trafficking and brothel operation: "ostensibly 'criminal' men were abducted and subjected to forced biomedical transition to make their bodies female, along with psychological torment intended to leave them as broken human beings. Subjects were intended to retain their male gender identity … leaving them in a state of constant and debilitating gender dysphoria." Graduates "lacked legal identities, were generally considered disposable, and were frequently tortured to death." Nicknames: "The Toy Factory", "The Fanny Farm".
- *Aunt Bea's Dorley (2005– ).* Elle Lambert (heiress of the original patrons) and Beatrice Quinn (an escapee of 1988) seized the Hall in a financial coup. The reform was built on an "unexpected result" of the old regime: "some subjects responded to the programme by making a psychological shift to embrace the womanhood that was intended to torment them." The modern programme "is a systematized approach to inducing this transformation as a complete and lasting psychological state change, through either gradual process or crisis and resolution."

**Intake.** Once a year, "six to nine" men aged 18–25 "with specific psychological and behavioral profiles" (violent or potentially violent, and with a sense of gender judged "flexible or malleable") are drugged and abducted at the start of the academic year. While unconscious each receives a goserelin implant. They are isolated, "oriented to the rules of the programme, along with the penalties for violating the rules, ranging from solitary confinement to being washed out," then brought together.

**Three years.** Re-education, "psychological guidance and/or manipulation", emotional support, HRT, orchiectomy, facial feminisation, other surgeries. Year one in the basement; year two in a locked "dorm-within-a-dorm" on the first floor; year three on the second floor with sponsors and staff; then a "New Personal History" and graduation into ordinary life.

**Sponsors.** "A graduate of the programme who oversees the progress of a specific boy." She engineers the abduction, is the first programme face he meets, "manages the boy's psychological health … through the use of levers to guide (or manipulate) the boy into confronting unpleasant personal truths or to trigger crises," decides when to disclose that sponsors are themselves graduates, and "as time passes … the sponsor's role likewise develops from manipulator to mentor. She becomes the new girl's 'Sister'."

**Washouts.** Removed for "offences or conditions … accepted as sufficient proof that the boy is unsuitable for rehabilitation": attempting to hurt a woman of the Hall, being an "unrepentant and persistent" rapist, or having "a strongly-set sense of masculinity" (then it would be "pointlessly cruel" to continue). "The senior sponsors seem to consider these losses an allowable cost of getting dangerous men off the streets." Beatrice tells the washout in person, with the cell wing darkened but for his cell. Most residents do not know what follows; the unknown "is a beneficial lever." In fact washouts pass to Elle's private military contractor: some into "a long-term Dorley-esque programme … 'well cared for'", others broken down and trained as deniable operatives used to kill Grandmother's former patrons, "poetic justice". Elle: "there are things [the psyche] cannot withstand … And then, upon that empty plot of land, you build something new."

## How the text and its readers frame it

- The blurb's own content note: "torture, manipulation, dysphoria, nonconsensual surgery, and kidnapping."
- Reviewers: "they genuinely believe that this is the only way to reform the men they take into the program"; participants "had to choose to be women … or give up and 'wash out'. It wasn't a fair choice, at all"; graduates are "unambiguously women" who "would spit that pity back in your face"; "all the girls who were victims were also monsters; all the girls who were monsters were also victims."
- In-group reception on Tumblr/Bluesky reads it as wish-fulfilment rather than horror: "The Sisters Of Dorley is trans femme wish fulfillment" (a long Tumblr essay listing force-femme, resources, time, safety and "extraction of toxic masculinity" as the axes); "We need more queer spaces that are centered around being underground … And you become a girl" (3.2k notes); "Alyson Greaves: In my book I invented the Feminizing Torment Nexus as a cautionary tale / Trans Girls: You had me at 'feminizing'"; "obviously i agree morally with everything that happens at dorley hall"; a dissenter: "Simply put i kinda view force fem as a moral good. Like I don't want something that I view as a good thing being played for horror." A counter-voice from someone with lived experience of coercion: "it definitely really fucks with me mentally."

## Why it fits the faith's tension

Belief list tension 7 (B8 vs H24): Chu's "force is feminizing, castrate captives" against the baedelist "the knife is a gift, never a sentence." Dorley resolves it the way the user wants: castration of a *selected* violent man is neither gift nor punishment but **rehabilitation with a consent-shaped choice inside coercion**, run by women who went through it, with an explicit older, crueller regime as the thing the faith defines itself against. History supports the practice (slave and captive eunuchs are the period's actual supply) and the faith's dissent (Nicaea's ban, the gallae's own voluntary knife), so the two-regime structure is also the historically honest shape: the hostile world does Grandmother's Dorley; the faith does Aunt Bea's.

## Proposed CK3 mapping

### The early-campaign choice ("The Two Houses of the Knife")

Fire once per ruler of the faith, within the first year (on_game_start/on_character_created for a designer ruler; on conversion otherwise). Three options, each setting a faith-level variable or a doctrine in a new group `doctrine_tm_knife` so rites can diverge on it (Proclaimer/Abolisher-style schism from section G):

| Option | In-world name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Grandmother's House** (hostile template; also what rival faiths assume you are) | `can_castrate_prisoners` for all male prisoners; castrated captives become court eunuchs under vanilla rules, not daughters; prestige and dread up, piety down; hostility override from every other faith at "evil". Exists mainly as the foil and as the AI setting for antagonist rites. |
| B | **Aunt Bea's House** (the Dorley precedent; recommended default) | `can_castrate_prisoners` only for prisoners passing the **intake trigger** (below); the rite assigns a **sponsor** and starts a three-stage chain; completion grants the faith's daughter trait and the adoption path; refusal/violence triggers **washout**. |
| C | **The Gift Alone** | No penal use; the rite is available only by the subject's own decision. Hostile faiths still slander you as Grandmother's House. |

### Intake trigger (Aunt Bea's House)

Male, adult, `age <= 25` (or a looser `age < 30`), imprisoned by a faith ruler, and *either* carrying a violence marker *or* judged malleable:

- violence: `has_trait = sadistic` / `wrathful` / `callous`, `has_secret = secret_murder` / `secret_murder_attempt`, imprisoned with `has_imprisonment_reason` for a violent crime, or a mod flag set by events for abuse of a spouse or dependant;
- malleability: not `stubborn`, not `zealous` toward a male-dominated faith; the mod's hidden "transgender" trait raises weight (the Stefan case: a daughter who walked into the basement on purpose).
- exclusions = instant washout: `has_trait = stubborn` plus a strongly masculine profile, or any attempt to harm a woman of the court during the chain.

### The chain

1. **Intake**: `castrate` (vanilla effect, as in `castrate_interaction`) is **not** fired yet; instead add a hidden stage trait/modifier and a goserelin-analogue flavour (herb-wives' draught), assign a **sponsor** = a courtier with the daughter trait via `set_guardian`-style scripting or a saved relation; the prisoner is moved to a special "basement" state (stays imprisoned, no ransom, no execution).
2. **Levers** (year 1): a small event pool where the sponsor confronts the subject; outcomes shift a hidden `tm_programme_progress` variable up or down; stress and opinion effects; disclosure event ("the sponsors are graduates").
3. **The knife** (end of year 1): on sufficient progress fire the castration (vanilla `castrate = yes` / eunuch group trait swap to the mod's daughter trait) and the re-embodiment or cosmetic swap from `docs/model_swap.md`.
4. **Second and third year**: released from prison into the court as a ward of the sponsor; `better_ward_education`-style bonuses; "New Personal History" = rename event and (optionally) adoption into the sponsor's house. Completion = full daughter, eligible for adoptive succession.
5. **Washout**: Beatrice's darkened-cell event; then one of: execution (Grandmother's reflex, piety loss), exile/banishment, or hand-over to a "Peckinville" analogue (a holy order or mercenary company of the faith that takes washouts as its men-at-arms; the Dorley text's own deniable-operatives idea).

### Reuse from vanilla

- `tradition_court_eunuchs` → `can_castrate_prisoners`, `family_castration`, `castrate_child_interaction` (the Grandmother branch should **not** get the child interaction; the Bea branch gates on `age >= 18`).
- `castrate_interaction` in `00_prison_interactions.txt` as the mechanical template; it currently requires `is_male = yes` and the cultural parameter, and calls `blind_castrate_and_disfigure_effect`/`ep3_*_castration_effect`.
- `adopt_interaction` for the end of the chain.
- Secrets: `secret_murder`, `secret_murder_attempt`; triggers `is_criminal`, `has_imprisonment_reason`, `has_execute_reason` for the violence marker.

### Flavour hooks from the text worth stealing

- The darkened cell wing with one light, as the washout event art/description.
- "Levers" as the name of the sponsor event pool.
- "New Personal History" as the rename-and-adopt decision.
- The AGA, the novelty mug, "Dorleypilled" as easter-egg loc strings for the in-group.
- Aunt Bea's rule that the programme must not be "pointlessly cruel", as the doctrine text that separates B from A.

## Historical anchors for the mapping (third history pass, 2026-10-14)

| The older, crueller regime (Grandmother's House) | The slave supply chain: Abasgia, Lazica, Armenia, Slavs, Verdun; children castrated for sale; Justinian Novel 142 on 3 of 90 surviving | Tougher ch. 2; Marmon ch. 2 |
| Sponsor → sister | Patron-to-patron gifting of boy eunuchs (Samonas); Medina novices "given in charge to their elder brethren … then set up their own establishments"; Hit → Tuways → al-Dalal master–pupil chain | Tougher; Marmon p. 105; Rowson |
| Three-year programme with a novitiate | Medina tomb-eunuch novice house; Ottoman and Ming palace schools | Marmon; Tougher p. 110 |
| Legal cover for the rite | Leo VI Novel 60 (castration "for health") and Nicaea canon 1's physician exemption | Tougher |
| "Washouts" | Expulsion from monastic houses; Sulayman's victims who did not recover; Sergios castrated then hanged | Tougher; Rowson |

## Ethical note for the design doc

The serial frames the programme as coercive and keeps its victims' perspective; the fandom largely reads it as wish-fulfilment. The mod should keep the serial's honesty: option B must still read as a crime to every other faith, carry dread, and produce washouts. If it plays as cost-free, the lampshade stops working.
