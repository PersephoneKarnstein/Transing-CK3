### Design consequences

1. **Homeland east of Rome.** Every ancient text that calls castrated men women is Syrian, Anatolian or Scythian; Roman texts say *semivir*. Byzantine eunuch culture and al-Jahiz carry it into 867. Place the culture's heritage in Anatolia/Syria/the steppe, not Latin Europe.
2. **Stage it as a heresy.** Hostile faiths should treat the rite as sodomy/counterfeit; that is the most period-true reception and gives natural hostility overrides and "Burned" endings.
3. **Castrate-as-woman is doctrine, not assumption.** Make it an explicit tenet or doctrine that rival faiths deny, since no historian says it was universal.
4. **Adoption is driven by childlessness.** The honest in-world logic: a castrated/celibate clergy cannot breed heirs, so the house chooses them (monastic succession). Matriline is the faith's law layered on that.
5. **No source bridges c. 250–867.** The faith must claim either secret survival of the Mother's cult or a revival; flavour text should say which.
6. **Borrow institutions, not identity concepts** (KA). Convent, guild, ocak, godparenthood and adelphopoiia are the shells; modern identity language stays in the design doc.

**Design consequences added**
10. **The medical loophole is the in-world legal cover.** Leo VI Novel 60 plus Nicaea's physician exemption: the rite is performed by a physician-priestess, recorded "for health", which is why the faith survives inside Byzantine law until the 11th century.
11. **Adoption carries the line; the vow carries the love.** Leo VI let eunuchs and childless women adopt, and adoption was a church rite by c. 800. Make mother→daughter adoption the succession mechanism and the sister-making vow a separate, higher-prestige union with no inheritance effect, exactly as the Byzantines had it.
12. **The Medina tomb-eunuchs are the sponsor template.** Novitiate under "elder brethren", then one's own establishment; offices pass to eunuchs by endowment; a celibate non-castrate may be admitted as a "functional eunuch" — a ready rule for cis women in the order.
13. **717 is the founding trauma** (Rowson 2003 dates Sulayman's order 717; earlier notes said 715). Sulayman's castration of the Medinan singers, the scribal-dot story and "we have become women in truth" give the faith a datable martyrdom and a six-step event chain (RW §6).
14. **Grow hostility with the era.** Byzantine tolerance of eunuch-angels and of brother-making both decay after c. 1050–1300; tie rival faiths' hostility level and the medical loophole's validity to the culture era.
15. **Al-Jahiz corrected (Ayalon read 2026-10-14, `hist_ayalon_eunuchs_caliphs.md`).** The sentence "castration transfers eunuchs from the realm of male characteristics to that of females" is not in Ayalon or al-Jahiz as quoted; it was a paraphrase in Karras. ## Corrections from the Byzantine reading (2026-10-18)
- The al-Jahiz line "transferral from the realm of male characteristics to that of females" is verbatim (Rowson 2003, p. 55, citing *Hayawan* 1:136), not a lost paraphrase; in the same passage al-Jahiz says eunuchs never become *mukhannathun* (research/isl_rowson_gender_irregularity.md).
- The Kecharitomene enclosure was "closed even to eunuchs", but its steward, priests and physician were eunuchs serving at the threshold (research/byz_thomas_hero_typika.md). What passed "in perpetuity" was the protectorship (*ephoreia*), not the property (G2 (g)).
- Galen's "third thing" is privative, not honourable (K IV 572, 585; research/byz_galen_on_semen.md).
- The faith's "castrates called women" evidence is Syrian; Anatolian galli dressed as women but are masculine in inscriptions (research/byz_roller_*.md, byz_latham_galli.md).
