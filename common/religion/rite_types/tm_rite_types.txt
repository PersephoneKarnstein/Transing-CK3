# The Galenic splinter: the rite that agrees with the Emperor (wave 3, docs/wave3/dorley.md; faith_design.md §12
# Byzantine revisions 2, Bhatt revisions 2). Leo VI's Novel 98 counts three kinds: men, women and eunuchs (Sidéris 2010,
# p. 89; Tougher 2008, p. 111: "some strange sex"). The accommodationist wing, not a neutral alternative: the main rite
# calls it the old cage with a new name. Its book, from c. 1100-07, is Theophylact of Ochrid's In Defence of Eunuchs
# (Mullett in Tougher (ed.) 2002, pp. 177-86; research/byz_tougher_eunuchs_antiquity_beyond.md row 12).
# Not created at the start (create = no). A Dorleyaian ruler founds it with tm_dl2_found_galenic_decision
# (create_rite_from_type, as vanilla does for quanzhen and daoxue); later rulers join it with tm_dl2_adopt_galenic_decision.
