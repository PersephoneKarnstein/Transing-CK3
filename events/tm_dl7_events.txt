# Crusader Dolls — Adelphai Dorleyaiou: Phrygia after Beatrike (docs/wave17/phrygia.md).
#   0001  the gold seal: the holder of Phrygia asks the throne to take the House's protection on itself
#   0010  the next appointment: Phrygia lost, the Mother of Mothers works the City for it
# In the tenth and eleventh centuries emperors gave monasteries to lay lords to hold (the charistike: research/
# byz_thomas_hero_typika.md §1.2, p. xxviii); the reformers' answer was an independent house with a powerful protector
# (p. 446), and Lips gave its ephoreia to the emperor "in perpetuity" (p. 1266). Basil II's Novel of 996 shows that a
# chrysobull could be annulled (hist_tougher_eunuch_byzantine.md): the seal is for a reign or so, not for ever.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
